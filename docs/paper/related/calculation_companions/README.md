# Calculation Companions

The Predictive Universe (PU) paper cites some long calculations, tables, certificates and proofs without printing them in full. This folder holds those files. Each one supports one or a few statements in the paper. The statement keeps its hypotheses and complete proof in the paper. The companion gives the expanded numbers, tables or records that the proof refers to.

## Layout

Each subfolder holds the companions of one part of the paper and is named after that part.

|:--|:--|--:|
| `section_02/` | Section 2, Foundational Principles | 2 |
| `section_05/` | Section 5, Complexity Thresholds | 1 |
| `section_06/` | Section 6, Complexity, Performance, and Adaptation Dynamics | 1 |
| `appendix_c/` | Appendix C, Necessity of Geometric Regularity | 1 |
| `appendix_e/` | Appendix E, Thermodynamic Limits and Horizon Entropy Area Law | 4 |
| `appendix_i/` | Appendix I, emergent gravity model | 4 and a proof folder |
| `appendix_k/` | Appendix K, Resolving Outstanding Puzzles | 2 and a proof folder |
| `appendix_l/` | Appendix L, Energy Accounting and Thermodynamic Consistency | 1 |
| `appendix_m/` | Appendix M, Perspectival Quantum Dynamics | 4 |
| `appendix_p/` | Appendix P, Philosophical Foundations | 1 |
| `appendix_s/` | Appendix S, Resource Cost and Stress-Energy | 1 |
| `appendix_v/` | Appendix V, Computational Programs | 2 |
| `appendix_x/` | Appendix X, PU and the Effective Action | 1 |
| `appendix_y/` | Appendix Y, Baryon Asymmetry | 3 |

The [contents](CONTENTS.md) list every file, the statement it supports and its size.
