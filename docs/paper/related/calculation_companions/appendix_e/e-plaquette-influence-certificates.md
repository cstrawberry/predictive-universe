# Plaquette influence calculations

These are the exact finite coefficient and factor data for Corollary E.4'.5a in [the thermodynamics and area-law section](../../../appendices/appendix_e_thermo_area_law.md). The statement, quantum–classical correspondence, coupling argument, normalization, gap comparisons and verification identities remain in that corollary. The present tables supply its finite inputs. All coefficients are exact integers or rationals; a tuple retains its printed order.

The variable is the corollary's $t\in[0,1]$. The residual factors have the form $g t^r(1-t)^s(1+t)^uQ_j(t)$. The residual denominator and probability prefactor are those explicitly defined in the corollary. The index $j$ selects the polynomial in the matching coefficient table. Ascending power coefficients mean $Q_j(t)=\sum_k c_{j,k}t^k$. Global spin reversal supplies the paired case, without changing the displayed factor data. Thus fifteen full-plaquette residuals and fifty-two clipped residuals suffice for all fifty-four and two-hundred-and-six cases respectively.
