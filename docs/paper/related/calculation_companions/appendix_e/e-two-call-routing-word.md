# Temporal word for the two-call routing fixture

This is the complete scalar word used by Theorem E.9.5f.51b in [the thermodynamics and area-law section](../../../appendices/appendix_e_thermo_area_law.md). The theorem retains its total compiler, full-operator preservation proof, exact primitive and metadata definitions, calendar, all pulse/hold/resource counts and conditional physical source boundary. The temporal data are separated here so that all 4,758 entries remain readable and independently addressable without interrupting those arguments.

The explanatory conventions below repeat the input definitions needed to read this exact word. Repetition and cancelling pairs are retained: each occurrence contributes its own charged pulse or hold. The formal source labels do not assert physical source authentication.

## Complete two-call temporal scalar word

**Complete fixture as an ordered mathematical word.** The original box is the single cell $(0,0,0)$, and the added spine consists of $(j,0,0)$ for $j=1,\ldots,5$. There are eighty-three logical slots. Slot zero is the sole original factor, of dimension two, at $(0,0,0)$. Slots 1–41 are external factors at $(1,0,0)$; slots 42–82 are external factors at $(2,0,0)$. Every original factor and every external factor remains distinct, even when their cells agree. The cells are the three integer coordinates just specified, rather than new tensor-factor identifications.

The three source-identity labels are the formal length-sixty-four words $0^{64}$, $1^{64}$ and $2^{64}$ for source, factor manifest and full word, respectively. They carry no source-authentication theorem. The two source calls have distinct labels $\mathsf U_0,\mathsf U_1$, the same formal identities, the declared fixture duration parameter and the declared original chronology. Insert $\mathsf U_0$ after scalar word entry 575 and $\mathsf U_1$ after scalar word entry 2,954. All other entries are the scalar entries below. Thus the skeleton has exactly 4,758 scalar positions and two unchanged source-call positions.
