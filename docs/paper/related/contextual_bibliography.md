# Bibliography of Related Ideas

This selective bibliography situates the Predictive Universe (PU) framework within neighboring work on process, prediction, self-reference, information, quantum theory, spacetime, gravitation, consciousness, and empirical method. The annotations identify conceptual or mathematical contact without treating proximity as a derivation of PU's branch conditions, bridge hypotheses, certificates, or empirical claims.

## 1. Epistemic and Process Starting Points

*Closest PU contact: Sections 1–3 and 14; Appendix P.*

* Descartes, R. (1641). *Meditationes de Prima Philosophia* (*Meditations on First Philosophy*). *(Historical source for the Cogito. PU takes the occurrence of an awareness/process as its epistemic starting point, then adds a finite operational representation by prediction, verification, and update.)*
* Leibniz, G. W. (1989). The principles of nature and grace, based on reason. In R. Ariew & D. Garber (Eds. & Trans.), *Philosophical Essays* (pp. 206–213). Hackett. (Original work published 1714). *(A historical relational and perspectival comparison; it does not supply the MPU construction or its finite-response minimization gates.)*
* Bergson, H. (1907). *L'Évolution créatrice*. Félix Alcan. (English translation: *Creative Evolution*, A. Mitchell, Trans., Henry Holt, 1911.) *(Process and becoming as philosophical context for PU's process-first vocabulary.)*
* Whitehead, A. N. (1929). *Process and Reality: An Essay in Cosmology*. Macmillan. (Corrected edition: D. R. Griffin & D. W. Sherburne, Eds., Free Press, 1978.) *(A major process-metaphysical comparison. PU's formal objects are finite response classes and predictive loops rather than Whiteheadian actual occasions.)*
