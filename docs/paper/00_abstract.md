# The Predictive Universe

## Abstract

The paper asks whether quantum behavior, spacetime, thermodynamics, and familiar physical structure can arise from finite predictive systems limited by self-reference and resources. It presents a mathematical core, conditional physical bridges, numerical applications, and open tests, while keeping their evidential status separate.
